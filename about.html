About NSC

Heavy civil. Built on experience.

A heavy civil contractor based in Richmond, BC, serving municipalities, school districts and builders across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley.

Our story

Founded on fifty years in the field.

Neil Smith and Dennis Frederickson started Neil Smith Construction in 2018. Neil brought over fifty years in excavation, heavy civil and agricultural work, from small backfill jobs to major highway projects with crews of fifty or more. Dennis brought decades of experience in residential and commercial construction and project management.

After Neil’s passing in 2022, Dennis took over leadership of the company and has guided it through steady growth: from utility upgrades and bulk excavation to school district programs, municipal sewer separation and specialized public facilities.

Today the company carries the standards it was founded on: quality work, delivered safely, on time and on budget.

How we deliver

Structured for major projects.

Every job is run by a full project team, from the first estimate to the final as-built.

  1. 01

    Estimating

    A dedicated estimating division prices tenders, prequalifications and change work for municipal, school district and private clients.

  2. 02

    Pre-construction

    Traffic management plans, local bylaws and Good Neighbour programs are reviewed and in place before we mobilize.

  3. 03

    Project management

    A Senior Project Manager on every project, with a dedicated Project Manager running it day to day.

  4. 04

    Field operations

    Site superintendents and foremen lead the crews, with Trimble GPS grade control and digital reporting on site.

  5. 05

    Quality and close-out

    Coordinators manage QA/QC records, RFIs, testing and as-builts through to handover.

  6. 06

    Safety

    COR certified, with a Construction Safety Officer and a toolbox meeting at the start of every shift.
