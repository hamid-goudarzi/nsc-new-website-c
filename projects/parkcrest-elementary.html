About the project

Civil works package C-028 for the new Parkcrest Elementary in Burnaby, including roads, a new parking area and full sewer and water upgrades.

Over almost three years we carried the civil package for the new school: excavation, storm and sanitary, fire and domestic water, and the road and frontage works around the site.

The job closed out with a new parking area, curb and gutter, sidewalks and paving.

Scope of work

  1. Bulk and detailed excavation for new construction
  2. Storm and sanitary sewer system upgrade
  3. Fire and domestic water upgrade
  4. Onsite and offsite concrete curb and gutter, sidewalk and driveway
  5. Road construction, asphalt overlay and new paving
  6. New parking area with concrete curb and gutter
